    Content copywriting

    Clear content and copywriting for your business

    I write practical website and marketing copy that helps visitors understand what you offer, who it is for and how to take the next step.

    Discuss a copywriting project

    What I do

    Copywriting starts with your goals, audience and existing materials. Together we agree which pages or content pieces to focus on.

    Website and service pages

    Explain your services in plain language and organize key information for your visitors.

    Landing page messaging

    Shape a focused page message around a specific offer, audience or action.

    Calls to action

    Make the next step clear with useful, direct prompts that fit the page purpose.

    Content editing

    Review existing copy for clarity, consistency and fit with your intended audience.

    My process

    Write with your audience in mind

    1. BriefDiscuss the business, audience, page purpose and desired next step.
    2. OutlineAgree on the message, structure and information needed.
    3. DraftWrite the agreed copy and share it for your review.
    4. RefineUse your feedback to prepare the final agreed content.

    Who this is for

    Small businesses and startups that need a clearer website, new service page copy, landing page messaging or a thoughtful review of existing text.

    Possible deliverables

    • Website page copy
    • Service page or landing page drafts
    • Calls to action and supporting messaging
    • Editing and content clarity recommendations

    Content copywriting FAQs

    What information do you need to write my copy?

    A project brief, details about your offer and audience, and any existing materials that can help explain your business.

    Can you edit copy I already have?

    Yes. We can agree whether you need a light edit, a content review or a more substantial rewrite.

    Can copywriting include SEO?

    Yes. We can coordinate copy with relevant page topics and SEO priorities as part of the agreed scope.

    How many revisions are included?

    The review and revision process will be agreed as part of your project scope before the writing begins.

    Need clearer words for your website?

    Share the page or message you are working on and the audience you want to reach.
