    Local SEO

    A practical local SEO checklist for small businesses

    Local visibility starts with accurate details and a useful experience for people deciding where to go or who to contact.

    When someone searches for a service nearby, they need more than a name in a results list. They want to know whether the business is relevant, where it works and how to take the next step. Local SEO is partly about making those details easy to verify.

    Keep your core business information consistent

    Check that your business name, phone number, address or service area, hours and website are accurate wherever customers may find you. Update important changes rather than leaving old details online. If you have a public business profile on a search platform, complete it honestly and use the fields that genuinely apply.

    Make your website useful to local visitors

    Explain the areas you serve and the services you provide there. A page for a specific location should contain real, helpful information, not just a swapped place name. Include practical details such as appointment availability, travel arrangements or the communities you cover when they matter to customers.

    Ask for feedback thoughtfully

    Reviews can help people understand what it is like to work with you. If you request feedback, make the request fair and straightforward. Never pressure customers to leave a particular rating or offer a reward in exchange for a positive review. Respond politely and avoid sharing private customer details.

    Check these foundations

    • Your website works on mobile and clearly displays contact options.
    • Location and opening details match your current business operations.
    • Service pages answer the questions local customers actually ask.
    • Maps, directions and booking links work as expected.
    • Any photos or claims accurately represent your business.

    Local search can change, and no checklist guarantees a particular position. Concentrate on trustworthy information and a smooth customer experience. Those improvements help people whether they discover you through a search engine, a recommendation or a direct visit.
