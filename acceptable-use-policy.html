Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Purpose

This policy explains what you may not do with Hostera services so that our network and customers stay safe.

Prohibited content

You may not host, store, or distribute child sexual abuse material, content that promotes violence or terrorism, content that infringes intellectual property rights, stolen data, or any content that is illegal.

Prohibited activity

You may not use our services for malware, phishing, spam, denial-of-service attacks, unauthorized access or scanning, fraud, or abusing other networks.

Platform operators

If you run a file sharing or user content platform, you must provide a way to report abuse, respond to valid notices promptly, and remove illegal content.

Enforcement

We may investigate violations, remove content, suspend or terminate services, and cooperate with lawful requests from authorities.

Reporting abuse

Report abuse to support@hostera.com with as many details as possible.
