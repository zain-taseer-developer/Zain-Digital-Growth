    Privacy Policy

    This page explains what information you may send through this website and how it is used.

    Information you provide

    The contact form asks for your name, email address, service interest and project details. The Write for Us form asks for your name, email address, organization, website, proposed article title, topic, pitch and an optional writing sample link. You may instead contact the listed email address. Please do not send sensitive personal, account or financial information through either form.

    How information is used

    Information you submit is used to read and respond to your enquiry, understand the project and discuss possible services or next steps. It is not used to make automated decisions about you.

    Website and form services

    This website is published through Netlify, and its contact and guest post forms are configured to receive submissions through the site. Netlify may process form data and technical request information under its own privacy terms. Review Netlify's current privacy information for details about its processing.

    Cookies and analytics

    The website files do not configure third-party analytics scripts. The site search uses a small script to read this site's sitemap and pages in your browser. The publication and form services may process technical information as described in their own policies.

    Retention and your questions

    Contact zainndevv@gmail.com to ask about information you have sent or to request its correction or deletion. Requests will be considered subject to applicable legal obligations.

    Policy updates

    This policy may be updated when the website or its data practices change. Please review this page for the current wording.
