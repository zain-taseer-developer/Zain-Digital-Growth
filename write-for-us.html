    Before you pitch

    Writing Guidelines

    To maintain quality and provide useful information to our readers, please follow these guidelines before submitting your article idea.

    • Original contentArticles must be original and not copied from other websites.
    • Word countAccepted articles are generally 800–1,200 words.
    • ReadabilityUse simple English, short paragraphs and clear sentences.
    • Useful headingsOrganize your article with clear H2 and H3 headings.
    • Reliable researchSupport important claims with trustworthy sources and relevant references.
    • Practical valueGive readers actionable information, examples, tips or solutions.
    • Helpful formattingUse bullet points and numbered lists wherever they improve clarity.
    • Image rightsOnly submit relevant images that you own or have permission to use.
    • Relevant linksInclude external links only when they are useful and contextual.
    • No disguised promotionArticles must not be advertisements. Copied, spun or misleading content will not be accepted.

    We do not guarantee backlinks, dofollow links or publication.

    Prepare your idea

    Before You Submit Your Pitch

    Submit an article idea first—not necessarily a complete article. It helps us understand what you want to write and the value it can offer readers.

    • A clear and interesting article title
    • A short summary of the proposed article
    • The main argument or key takeaway
    • Three to five main points you plan to cover
    • Relevant sources, research or examples
    • A short author biography
    • Published articles or a writing portfolio, if available

    Editorial review

    Our Editorial Process

    Every pitch is reviewed with our readers and editorial standards in mind.

    • Every pitch is subject to editorial review.
    • We may decline topics that do not fit our website.
    • We may request changes or additional information.
    • Submitting does not guarantee publication.
    • We retain editorial control over titles, formatting and final publication decisions.
    • Contributors must have the rights to submit their content and images.
    • Previously published or duplicate articles may be declined.

    FAQs

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who can contribute?

    Anyone with relevant knowledge, practical experience or useful insights in our accepted categories can submit a pitch.

    What topics do you accept?

    We accept topics related to web development, SEO, digital marketing, WordPress, WooCommerce, AI, technology, blogging and online business.

    How long should an article be?

    Accepted articles will generally be between 800 and 1,200 words, depending on the subject.

    Can I submit a complete article?

    We recommend submitting a pitch first. If the idea is approved, our editorial team may request a complete draft.

    How will I know if my pitch is accepted?

    We will use the email address provided in your submission to contact you about relevant next steps.

    Can I include links in my article?

    Relevant and useful links may be included. Promotional, spammy or irrelevant links are not accepted.

    Does submitting a pitch guarantee publication?

    No. All submissions are reviewed, and publication depends on editorial approval.
