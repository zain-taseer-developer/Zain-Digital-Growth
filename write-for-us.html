    Write for Us

    Have a practical lesson or perspective that could help a small business grow online? Send a guest post pitch for consideration.

    What makes a good pitch?

    Suggest a clear, original article that gives small business owners practical advice about websites, search, content or digital marketing.

    • Share a focused topic and useful takeaways.
    • Send original work that has not been published elsewhere.
    • Keep the pitch relevant, accurate and easy to understand.
    • Include a writing sample or portfolio if you have one.

    Sending a pitch does not guarantee publication. Please do not include sensitive personal information.
