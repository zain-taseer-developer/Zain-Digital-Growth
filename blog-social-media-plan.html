    Social media

    Build a social media plan you can actually keep up with

    A useful social presence does not have to mean posting every day. Start with a manageable routine that gives people a reason to pay attention.

    Many small businesses begin social media with good intentions, then struggle to keep the schedule going. A plan works better when it fits the time and people you actually have, rather than an ideal week that rarely happens.

    Decide what you want the channel to do

    Choose one or two realistic goals, such as answering common questions, showing recent work or helping people learn about a service. The goal can shape what you share and how you judge whether the effort is worthwhile. Not every post needs to sell directly.

    Pick a few repeatable themes

    Content themes reduce the pressure to invent a new idea every time. For example, a local service business might share helpful tips, behind-the-scenes process, customer questions and occasional project updates. Keep the themes connected to what your audience cares about and what you can explain honestly.

    Set a sustainable schedule

    Start with a frequency you can maintain for a month. One thoughtful post each week is more useful than a burst of daily posts followed by silence. Choose a regular time to gather ideas, draft captions and prepare images. Reuse an idea across formats when it makes sense, but tailor it to the platform rather than copying without context.

    Make it easier to follow through

    • Keep a simple list of questions customers ask.
    • Capture photos or short notes while work is already happening.
    • Write captions in batches and check details before publishing.
    • Review which posts prompt useful conversations, not just views.
    • Adjust the schedule when it stops being practical.

    Social media is one way to stay connected, not the whole marketing plan. Keep your website and contact details current so interested people have a reliable place to learn more, even when algorithms or platform features change.
