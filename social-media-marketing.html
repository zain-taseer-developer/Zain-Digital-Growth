What I do

Audience and channel planning

Clarify who you want to reach and which social channels suit your business and capacity.

Content themes

Review and refinement

Review the content together and refine the wording to match your brand and project scope.

Social media marketing FAQs

Which social media platforms do you support?

We can discuss the channels your audience uses and agree which ones fit the project. Platform selection depends on your goals and capacity.

Do you publish posts for clients?

This page describes planning and content support. Any publishing or account-management tasks must be discussed and explicitly included in the agreed scope.

Can you write posts in my brand voice?

Yes. I will ask about your business and preferred tone, then share drafts for your review and feedback.

How often should my business post?

There is no single schedule that fits every business. A practical cadence depends on your audience, goals and the time you can give to content.
