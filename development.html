    Website Development

    Website development built around your business goals

    I plan and build clear, responsive websites that help small businesses explain their offer and make it easy for visitors to take the next step.

    Discuss a website project

    What I do

    Website work can include a new small-business website or improvements to an existing one. The exact scope depends on your audience, content and project priorities.

    Website planning and structure

    Organize key pages, navigation and calls to action so visitors can understand what you offer.

    Responsive page development

    Create or update front-end pages with a clear layout that adapts to common screen sizes.

    Content and contact paths

    Place service information and contact options where visitors can find and use them.

    Review and handover

    Check the agreed pages, incorporate feedback within the project scope and share the finished work.

    My process

    From brief to handover

    The work is planned with you, with clear checkpoints before delivery.

    1. UnderstandDiscuss goals, audience, required pages and existing materials.
    2. PlanConfirm page structure, priorities and the agreed scope.
    3. BuildCreate the pages and share progress for your feedback.
    4. Review and deliverMake agreed refinements and hand over the completed work.

    Who this is for

    Small businesses, independent professionals and startups that need a new website or a clearer, more usable version of an existing site.

    Website development FAQs

    Can you improve an existing website?

    Yes. We can discuss the current site, what is working and which improvements fit the project scope.

    Will my website work on mobile screens?

    Responsive layout is part of the development approach, and the agreed pages are reviewed at common screen sizes.

    Can you help with the words on my website?

    Yes. Content copywriting is available as a related service, and we can include it in the agreed scope.
