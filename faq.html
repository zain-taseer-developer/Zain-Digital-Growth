    Services and project questions

    Which services do you offer?

    I offer website development, SEO, social media marketing and content copywriting.

    Can I start with just one service?

    Yes. You can ask about one service or discuss a combination that fits your project and priorities.

    How does a project begin?

    Send a short description of your business, goals and current needs through the contact form. We can discuss a suitable scope and next steps.

    Do you guarantee search rankings or marketing results?

    No. Search and marketing outcomes depend on factors outside any one provider's control. The work focuses on the agreed deliverables and clear recommendations.

    How are project scope and pricing decided?

    Project scope and pricing are discussed and agreed before work begins, based on the work requested.

    Can you work with an existing website or content?

    Yes. Share the current site or content when you get in touch, and we can discuss whether it fits the work you need.

    Still have a question?

    Send a note about your project and I will help clarify what the next step could be.
